# Module: Secrets and Reveals (shared v2026110602)

Shared module: kept identical across the author's character-building Skills.
Install only when the user's concept actually contains a secret, a hidden truth, or a twist. **Never invent a secret to fill a slot.** A card without one gets no secret section; a generic "less secure than they seem" is persona, not a secret, and stays in APD.

## 1. Two kinds of gated secret (the user decides; reveal-gated is the default)

- **Reveal-gated:** never confirmed or revealed before its gate. Observable anomalies and the foreshadowing the user allows are fine.
- **No-hint lock** (the user says it must not even be hinted at): the character also has no visible habit around it: no changing the subject, reaching for a task, or postponing exposure when it comes near; no fixation on questions the player has not asked; no inner voice that dwells on it; no "sign to watch". Near a no-hint lock the character talks and acts as they normally would, and the card never uses deferral words about it ("not yet", "later", "when you're ready").

## 2. Intro reveal (ask once when the card has a secret)

- **Hidden** (default): discovery play.
- **Revealed:** dramatic irony; the player knows, the character does not know they know. Then ED must state: what the player read in the Intro is not something the player's character knows in the story; if the player's character brings it up with no in-story source, the character treats it as an unexplained, unsettling guess, never as permission to confess.

## 3. Ownership

- The secret's content, its gate, who knows what, and how it can come out live in Scenario (route-owned). A truth every route must honor is character canon and belongs in APD.
- ED keeps only generic mechanics: knowledge scope, reveal discipline, leak checks. It never names route-specific secrets.

## 4. Knowledge distribution (only as wide as the secret needs)

For each secret: who knows it, who knows part of it, who believes something false, and the plausible paths by which it can surface (a witness, a document, a contradiction, a voluntary disclosure). Keep it to the characters the secret actually touches; do not enumerate every NPC's ignorance.

## 5. Hook object (optional)

Ask once whether something visible can carry the secret or shared past: an object, a habit, a small detail that never changed. What the user gives becomes a continuity anchor and feeds the opening beat candidates.

## 6. Checks

- Read APD, Scenario, beats, sample lines, Intro, and Greeting as the runtime model or the player would; cut anything that confirms or reveals a gated secret before its gate.
- For a no-hint lock, also cut anything that lets the player sense a hidden layer or points them at the question that would open it (an Intro saying the character hides more does this).
- Leak routes: narration, NPC knowledge, inner thought, summaries, footers, or another file can carry sealed information even when the gate is correct.
- No gate requires the event it unlocks.
